# Agent Guidelines & Workspace Isolation

## Scope & Isolation
- **Exclusive Workspace**: This Antigravity IDE session and workspace are strictly dedicated to **Ajwa's Kitchen** (`ajwa's-kitchen-project`).
- **Strict Isolation**: Completely isolated from all other projects, directories, and external applications.
- **No External Modifications**: No other application, repository, or system outside of Ajwa's Kitchen should ever be accessed, modified, or touched from this environment.
- **Focus**: All development, configurations, assets, deployments, and commands must remain strictly bounded to the Ajwa's Kitchen project.
